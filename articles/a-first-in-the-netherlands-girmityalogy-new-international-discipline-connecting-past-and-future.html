A First in the Netherlands: 'Girmitiyalogy' – New International Discipline Connecting Past and Future

By GGLASC Editorial · 21 June 2025GGLASC संपादकीय द्वारा · 21 June 2025

On Saturday, 5 July, the very first international Hybrid Meet & Greet on Girmitiyalogy will take place in the Netherlands. Girmityalogy is a new academic discipline, recently established in India, and rapidly gaining significance worldwide. Journalists, researchers, students, and anyone interested in heritage, diaspora, and colonial history are welcome to attend this unique event.

New Science, Old Stories

Girmityalogy is emerging at respected Indian universities such as the University of Delhi and the University of Lucknow. The discipline focuses on the history and shared heritage of the Girmityas – people of Indian origin who migrated to places like the Caribbean during colonial times, often under coercion. This field builds a bridge between the past, identity, and community, and offers deep relevance for today’s social issues.

A Layered and Connecting Programme

This event offers more than academic insight. Attendees will meet the initiators of this movement, including leading scholars (names to be announced). The afternoon will feature a live performance by Raj Mohan, showcasing traditional Girmitiya songs. Following the music, a communal dinner will offer participants a chance to engage in conversation and build meaningful connections. The event is accessible to all, with options to attend either in person or online.

A Call to Engage

Our goal is simple yet powerful: to amplify the voices of the Girmitiyas, turning remembrance into a movement that educates, empowers, and inspires the world. By reconnecting with shared history and collective heritage, space is created for a new, common starting point—a foundation for self-aware communities that honour their past and shape their future.

This Meet & Greet invites more than just listening. It calls for reflection, dialogue, and movement.

Also, Dr Shardhanand Harinandan Singh's new book will be launched at this hybrid Meet & Greet International Conference.
